Content has to beat its metadata
Ridge financial values improve over the simpler filing-age control in this cohort. LSTM financial content does not show that reliable increment. Frozen FinBERT embeddings show no stable extra benefit over their metadata controls; adding them to the financial LSTM increases loss. This is evidence about one pooled-embedding procedure and cohort, not a universal claim about language or deep learning.

The application
A portfolio holder wants to understand concentration and compare transparent rules for adjusting equity exposure. Enter a stock basket, inspect estimated risk, then compare historical decisions with their costs and consequences.
The research question
Does forecast-based exposure control improve risk-target tracking over a historical-volatility rule, and what return, turnover and cash-allocation tradeoffs accompany it?
What is implemented
Correlation-aware portfolio risk, per-holding risk contributions, buy-and-hold, fixed-equity, historical-risk and forecast-risk simulations. The matched study freezes constant exposure from earlier validation forecasts; the preliminary study retains its fixed-half comparator. Same stock basket, execution assumptions and cost rate; wealth and weights drift between trades.
Historical V2 forecasts supply Ridge, boosting and LSTM comparators. The separate full-cohort research now includes Timestral 5M; the replay offers both the matched financial/text study and preliminary V2 inputs, with separate universes and model menus.
What is still a research task
Finalize the evaluation protocol, resolve remaining identity/coverage limitations and review pretraining overlap before opening the sealed 2026 time holdout. No universal model advantage is claimed.
Data and evaluation limits
Interactive replay: either 19 matched held companies or the separately labeled preliminary 30-stock study. Full research: 491 retained histories from 500 reconstructed reference-date issuers, including financial firms in market-only experiments. Neither panel reconstructs historical index membership at every date.
Existing test years have already been inspected. This application is retrospective exploratory research. Presets and interactive configurations are scenarios, not preselected evidence of success.
Signals form after close and execute at the following close. The matched study aligns single-stock targets to post-execution holding windows; preliminary V2 experiments retain their earlier implementation lag. Cash yield is zero; costs are proportional; no taxes, liquidity model or broker execution.
Earlier experiments
V1–V3 investigated forecast accuracy and the incremental value of accounting variables. They remain preliminary forecasting evidence. Their scores are not portfolio backtest results.
Higher financial coverage did not establish stable incremental value from full accounting values. Those observations motivate testing the decision value of forecasts rather than assuming more inputs help.
AI's role
Explain server-computed evidence and limitations. The assistant does not select trades, invent market news or infer unseen future outcomes. Simulation and export work without an AI key.
